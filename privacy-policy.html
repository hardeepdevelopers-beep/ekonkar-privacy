Ek Onkar – Gurbani & Simran Privacy Policy Android package: com.hs.ekonkar Effective date: 26 October 2026 Operator: HS Digital Labs Privacy contact: hardeep.developers@gmail.com This policy explains how the Android app handles information when you listen to Gurbani, use Simran, read devotional content, view wallpapers or receive notifications. Some information stays on your device; network services and integrated SDKs also process information. An app account is not required, and this version does not offer app-account registration. Information stored on your device The app uses Android preferences, DataStore and files to retain settings and provide offline or resumed experiences. This includes appearance and notification preferences, stored privacy choices, local premium/ad-free flags, Simran progress and haptic settings, reminder configuration, playback settings/state, notification deduplication records and cached content such as channel metadata, images and reading files. The reviewed app code does not upload your locally stored Simran count or round identifier as a devotional record. Optional analytics may separately report that a feature such as Simran was used. Local settings and caches are not the same as SDK identifiers or server logs described below. Downloaded wallpapers saved to your Pictures folder and files you share are separate from the app cache. Android backup is enabled in the app manifest, so eligible app data may also be backed up or restored through your device's backup service, depending on Android settings and backup rules. 2. Information transmitted to third-party services Advertising — Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform The app supports banner, native and interstitial ads. When ads are eligible to run, Google's advertising SDK can process IP addresses, device/advertising identifiers, app and device information, ad interactions and diagnostic information. IP addresses may be used to estimate approximate location. These data support ad delivery and measurement, service operation and fraud prevention. Consent choices and applicable requirements affect advertising behavior; non-personalized ads do not mean that no data is processed. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to manage advertising consent and privacy choices where required. Premium/ad-free eligibility suppresses ad requests; it does not by itself disable other services in this policy. Firebase configuration, notifications and identifiers Firebase Remote Config retrieves configuration, including feature/ad settings. Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registers an app installation for notifications. These services use installation or registration identifiers and technical request information such as IP address, app/device details and SDK information to operate and secure their services. FCM auto-initialization and token registration are enabled independently of optional notification delivery. Turning off a reminder or denying notification permission does not necessarily stop Firebase registration or all associated network processing. Notification settings determine delivery and topic subscriptions; the SDK manages the registration token. Optional Firebase analytics and diagnostics Analytics and crash collection are disabled by default in the manifest and runtime privacy policy. Collection can be enabled by an existing stored preference; choices may persist across app updates or backup restoration. Performance collection also follows the stored diagnostics preference. When enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase processes app interactions, screen/session activity, app/device information and installation identifiers for usage analysis. App-defined events describe feature categories and actions, such as opening content or using playback; they do not send the local Simran count. Analytics advertising-ID collection and default advertising-storage/personalization permissions are disabled in the app configuration. This restriction is separate from AdMob's identifier processing. When diagnostics are enabled, Crashlytics processes crash reports, sanitized non-fatal errors, stack traces and app/device/installation information. Firebase Performance Monitoring can process timing, network-request metadata, response codes, payload sizes and technical performance data. These services help diagnose failures and improve reliability. Turning diagnostics off invokes deletion of unsent crash reports; it does not automatically delete reports already received by Google. OneSignal The OneSignal SDK is initialized in the app. Legacy OneSignal push delivery is disabled by the current notification coordinator, but disabling delivery is not equivalent to disabling SDK initialization or all data processing. OneSignal can process subscription/device identifiers, push tokens, IP-derived location, device/app attributes, session activity and notification-related information according to its SDK behavior and applicable settings. Its privacy information is linked below. Wallpapers, reading content, radio and external pages The wallpaper feature reads public wallpaper metadata and images from Supabase. The reviewed wallpaper integration does not create a user account or upload your images or local preferences to a Supabase profile. Supabase and its infrastructure receive network request data such as IP address, requested resource, request headers and access times. The app also downloads reading/content files from Firebase Storage and connects to content and streaming providers, including SikhNet and SGPC. Those providers receive the technical connection information needed to deliver the requested content, including IP address and requested resource. Opening a browser, Custom Tab or embedded page may expose additional information to that site under its own policy. Sharing through WhatsApp or another app transfers the selected file/content to the app you choose and is subject to that service's practices. We do not ask you to provide a name, email address or phone number to use the app. If you contact the operator voluntarily, the operator receives the contact information and message you provide to respond to your request. 3. Purposes, recipients and sharing Information is processed to deliver content and playback, retain preferences, provide reminders and notifications, display and measure ads, obtain configuration, maintain security, prevent fraud, investigate failures and respond to support or privacy requests. Optional analytics supports understanding feature usage. Recipients include Google/Firebase/AdMob, OneSignal, Supabase and their service infrastructure, the content providers you access, and recipients/services you choose when sharing. Advertising-related processing can be considered sharing under privacy laws and Google Play definitions. The reviewed app code does not send a local Simran-history database to advertisers. Provider processing may take place outside your country. Provider terms, contractual arrangements and applicable law govern their processing and international transfers. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or to address security or legal claims; this is not a claim that such a disclosure has occurred. Retention Local preferences and progress generally remain until you reset them, clear app storage or uninstall, subject to Android backup/restoration. Caches may be evicted or replaced; temporary wallpaper-share files older than 24 hours are cleaned up when the share routine runs, rather than on a guaranteed background deletion schedule. Exported wallpapers and copies shared to other apps can remain after uninstall and must be managed separately. Data already sent to service providers is retained under their product settings, service policies and applicable legal requirements. The operator has not specified a universal retention period for all provider data, and this policy does not promise immediate deletion of all logs or backups. Contact the operator for information about a particular record or service. Support correspondence is retained as needed to address the request and applicable obligations. 7.
